Williams F1

61 models · provisional estimate $1,230–$2,745

Williams-liveried 1:43 die-casts from the Grove team — the Rothmans, Winfield and BMW years, carrying Damon Hill, Jacques Villeneuve, Ralf Schumacher, Juan Pablo Montoya and Jenson Button. These are the standard (non-numbered) pieces; Williams limited editions are gathered in the Special & Limited Editions lot. Provisional lot estimate $1,230–$2,745.
